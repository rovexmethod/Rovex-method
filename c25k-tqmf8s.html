Before You Start

FUNDAMENTALS

  • Run 3–4x per week, with rest or easy days between sessions
  • Cadence: don't force a number — let it rise naturally with speed. If you're overstriding, aim for shorter, quieter steps
  • Most of your runs should feel easy — you should be able to hold a conversation
  • Rest days are training days. Your body adapts during recovery, not during the run itself
  • Bad run > no run. Showing up consistently matters more than any single session
  • It's normal to walk. Every interval here is designed with walk breaks built in
